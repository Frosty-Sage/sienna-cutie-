Dear Miss Little's Feet —

I made this because some people become important in such quiet ways that you don't notice it happening at first.

One day they're just a person.
And then somehow they're the reason certain songs sound better.
The reason red roses seem prettier.
The reason you save cat pictures you'll probably never send.

You became one of my favorite little coincidences.
The kind that slips into ordinary days and makes them feel softer.

And I think my favorite thing about you is that you probably don't realize the effect you have on people.
How easy it is to smile because of something you've said.
How easily you stay on someone's mind.
How naturally you make a place feel warmer simply by being there.

So I made this tiny corner for you.
A place full of flowers, cats, and small reminders that somebody thinks you're rather wonderful.

And if life ever feels heavy,
or if you forget, even for a moment, how lovely you are—
come back here.

The roses will still be blooming.
The cats will still be waiting.
And tucked between all of it,
you might find all the things I've never been quite brave enough to say out loud.

♡

P.S. You're still only 5 feet tall.
That part remains scientifically undeniable.
Sorry, Miss Little 5 Feet. ♡
